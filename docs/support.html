Frequently Asked Questions

I bought a skin pack on a new phone and it is locked.

Open Skins (or Settings) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

My high scores disappeared. Can they be restored?

The local top 20 is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it; there is no cloud backup. Scores submitted to Game Center or Google Play Games stay on their leaderboard.

Does Kačeny need an internet connection?

No. The game is fully playable offline. Only purchases and the online leaderboard need a connection.
